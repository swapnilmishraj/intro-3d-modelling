# Lesson 2 — Nameplate / Keychain

## Goal

Make a simple nameplate (or a keychain).
You will use a box and text.
You will align and group.

## Time

15–25 minutes

## What you need

- Tinkercad
- A saved design from Lesson 1 (optional)

## Steps

1. Open Tinkercad.
2. Create a new design.
3. Drag a **Box** onto the workplane.
4. Set a simple size (in mm). Example:
   1. Length: 60
   2. Width: 20
   3. Height: 4
5. Drag **Text** onto the workplane.
6. Click the text and type your name (or initials).
7. Set the text height. Example: 2 mm.
8. Move the text on top of the box.
9. Resize the text so it fits on the box.
10. Select the box and the text.
11. Click **Align** and center the text.
12. Click **Group**.

Optional: add a keychain hole

13. Drag a **Cylinder** onto the workplane.
14. Change it to **Hole**.
15. Set the hole size. Example:
    1. Diameter: 5 mm
    2. Height: 10 mm (taller than your plate)
16. Move the hole near one end of the nameplate.
17. Select the nameplate and the hole.
18. Click **Align** if needed.
19. Click **Group** to cut the hole out.

## Common mistakes and fixes

- Text is not touching the base: Lower the text until it sits on the box.
- Text is too thin: Increase text height (try 2–3 mm).
- The hole did not cut through: Make the hole height taller, then group.
- The hole is too close to the edge: Move it inward so it has a “rim”.
