# Lesson 5 — Export and Print Checks

## Goal

Export an STL.
Do quick checks so the model is more likely to print well.

## Time

10–20 minutes

## What you need

- Tinkercad
- One finished design (nameplate, house, or stamp)

## Steps

1. Open your design in Tinkercad.
2. Do a quick “print-ready” check:
   1. Is it one solid object? (Group parts if needed.)
   2. Is anything floating in the air?
   3. Are parts connected to the main body?
   4. Is it thick enough? (Try not to make super-thin parts.)
3. Check the size:
   1. Tinkercad uses **mm**.
   2. A keychain is often 40–70 mm wide.
4. Put it flat on the workplane:
   1. If needed, rotate it so the bottom is flat.
   2. Lower it until it sits on the workplane.
5. Rename the design so it is easy to find later.

### Part B: Export Your Design as an STL File

Now let's save your design in the format 3D printers need!

9. Look at the top-right of the Tinkercad screen
10. Click the **Export** button
11. A menu will appear — choose **".STL"**
12. Your computer will download the file (it goes to your Downloads folder)
13. **That's it!** You now have a file ready for 3D printing! 🎉

### Important Things to Remember

**About 3D Printing:**
- 3D printers build objects layer by layer (like stacking very thin pancakes)
- Each layer is super thin — usually 0.1-0.3 mm!
- The printer melts plastic and draws each layer, then moves up and does the next one

**About STL Files:**
- STL is the standard file format for 3D printing
- It tells the printer the exact shape of your object
- Think of it like a recipe that the printer follows

## Common Problems and How to Fix Them

**Problem:** I can't find the Export button!
- **Fix:** Click on the empty workplane first (to deselect everything), then look at the top-right again.

**Problem:** My model is made of many separate parts.
- **Fix:** Hold **Shift**, click all the parts to select them, then click **Group** at the top.

**Problem:** A hole didn't cut through properly.
- **Fix:** Click **Ungroup**, select the hole shape, make it taller (so it goes all the way through), then **Group** again.

**Problem:** Parts of my model are too thin and might not print.
- **Fix:** Make thin parts thicker — aim for at least 1.5-2 mm. Click on the part and drag the resize handles.
