# Introduction to 3D Modelling (Micro‑Course)

This is a short, beginner-friendly course.
It teaches a 9th grader the basics of 3D modelling.
We use Tinkercad in a web browser.

## What this course is

- Short lessons (10–25 minutes)
- Simple steps you can follow
- Small designs you can export as STL
- No installs needed (just a browser)

## What you need

- A computer (macOS is fine)
- A mouse or trackpad
- Internet
- A free Tinkercad account
