# Lesson 4 — Holes and Cutouts

## Goal

This is where 3D modelling gets really cool!

By the end of this lesson, you will:
- Understand how "holes" work in Tinkercad
- Use holes to cut shapes out of solid objects
- Make a keychain with a perfect hole
- Create a stamp with cut-out letters

## Time

15–25 minutes

## What you need

- Your Tinkercad account

## Understanding Holes (Important Concept!)

**Here's the big idea:**
- In Tinkercad, every shape can be either **Solid** or **Hole**
- A **Solid** shape is normal (filled in)
- A **Hole** shape is like a cookie cutter — it cuts away from solid shapes
- When you **Group** a solid shape and a hole shape together, the hole cuts out that part!

**Think of it like:** Using a cookie cutter on dough. The cutter (hole) removes that shape from the dough (solid).

Let's try it!

## Steps

### Part A: Make a Keychain with a Perfect Hole

Let's make a keychain and cut a hole in it for a key ring!

3. Create a new design in Tinkercad
4. Make the keychain base:
   - Add a **Box**: 50 × 20 × 4 mm
   - (Optional) Add **Text** with your name on top
   - (Optional) Align and group the text with the box
5. Now let's add the hole! Drag a **Cylinder** onto the workplane
6. Click on the cylinder to select it
7. Look at the **Inspector** panel (usually on the right)
8. Click where it says **"Solid"** and change it to **"Hole"**
   - Notice the cylinder turns gray/transparent!
9. Set the hole size:
   - **Diameter:** 5 mm (this will fit most key rings)
   - **Height:** 10 mm (taller than the keychain so it cuts all the way through)
10. Move the hole cylinder to one corner of your keychain
    - Leave some space from the edges (you need a "rim" or it'll break)
11. Hold **Shift** and select both the keychain base and the hole
12. Click **Group**
13. **Magic!** The hole cuts right through! ✨

**Now you can see through the hole!**

### Part B: Make a Custom Stamp (Cut-Out Letters)

This is super cool — you'll make a stamp where the letters are carved IN!

14. Drag a **Box** onto the workplane for your stamp base
15. Make it stamp-sized: **35 × 35 × 6 mm**
    - Make it thick enough (at least 6mm) or the stamp will be too fragile
16. Drag **Text** onto the workplane
17. Type 1-3 letters (your initials work great!)
18. Click on the text to select it
19. In the **Inspector** panel, change it from **"Solid"** to **"Hole"**
20. Set the text height to **2-3 mm**
    - Not too deep or it'll be hard to print
21. Position the text on top of the stamp base
22. Lower the text slightly so it pushes INTO the base (not all the way through!)
    - Drag the cone downward until about half the text is below the surface
23. Hold **Shift** and select both the stamp base and the text hole
24. Click **Group**
25. **Boom!** You've got carved letters! 🎨

**Cool, right?** The letters are now indented into the stamp. If you had an ink pad, you could use this as a real stamp!

## Common mistakes and fixes

- Nothing cuts out: Make sure the cutting shape is set to **Hole**.
- The hole did not go all the way through: Make the hole taller, then group.
- Letters look messy: Use fewer letters and make them bigger.
- The stamp is too thin: Make the base 6–8 mm tall.
