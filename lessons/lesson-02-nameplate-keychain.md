# Lesson 2 — Nameplate / Keychain

## Goal

You're going to make something cool that's actually yours! 

By the end of this lesson, you will:
- Create a personalized nameplate with your name on it
- Learn how to add and position text in 3D
- (Optional) Add a hole to turn it into a keychain
- Have your first real 3D design ready to print!

## Time

15–25 minutes (totally worth it!)

## What you need

- Your Tinkercad account
- The skills from Lesson 1 (moving, aligning, grouping)

## Steps

### Part A: Create the Base Plate

1. Log into Tinkercad and click **Create New Design**
2. Find the **Box** shape on the right panel and drag it onto the workplane
3. Click on the box to select it
4. Look for the size measurements that appear
5. Change the size by typing in these numbers:
   - **Length:** 60 mm (how wide it is)
   - **Width:** 20 mm (how deep it is)
   - **Height:** 4 mm (how tall/thick it is)

**Tip:** These sizes work well, but you can make yours bigger or smaller!

### Part B: Add Your Name

6. Find the **Text** shape in the shapes panel and drag it onto the workplane
7. Click on the text shape
8. In the text box that appears, delete the example text and type YOUR NAME (or just your initials)
9. Look for the "Height" setting and change it to **2 mm** (this makes the letters thick enough)
10. Drag your name on top of the box
11. Make the text bigger or smaller so it fits nicely on the box:
    - Click the text
    - Drag the corner handles to resize it

### Part C: Center and Combine

12. Hold **Shift** and click both the box and the text (both should be highlighted)
13. Click the **Align** button at the top
14. Click the center dot on the align menu to center your text perfectly
15. With both still selected, click **Group**
16. **Awesome!** You now have a nameplate!

### Part D (Optional): Add a Keychain Hole

Want to turn your nameplate into a keychain? Let's add a hole!

17. Drag a **Cylinder** onto the workplane
18. Click the cylinder and look for the **Inspector** panel
19. Change it from "Solid" to **"Hole"** (you'll see it turn transparent/gray)
20. Set the hole size:
    - **Diameter:** 5 mm (this is the width of the hole)
    - **Height:** 10 mm (make it taller than your plate so it cuts all the way through)
21. Drag the hole cylinder to one end of your nameplate
22. Position it so there's a good "rim" of material around it (not too close to the edge)
23. Hold **Shift** and select both your nameplate and the hole
24. Click **Align** if you want to center it
25. Click **Group** — the hole will cut through!

**Congrats!** You just made your first personalized 3D design! 🎉

## Common mistakes and fixes

- Text is not touching the base: Lower the text until it sits on the box.
- Text is too thin: Increase text height (try 2–3 mm).
- The hole did not cut through: Make the hole height taller, then group.
- The hole is too close to the edge: Move it inward so it has a “rim”.
